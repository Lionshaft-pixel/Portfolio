My Greatest Weakness is Eye Contact

19 June, 2026 | 3:58 AM

I genuinely think my crush gave me trust issues. Not Intentionally? Probably? I don't know. See? That's the problem.

I don't know.

And I hate not knowing things. A few months ago, I was a pretty simple guy. If something happened, I'd try to understand it. If something broke, I'd fix it. If something didn't make sense, I'd keep digging until I found an answer.

Then one day my crush blocked me on Instagram.

And to this day, I still don't know why. That's it. No dramatic ending. No explanation. No arguments. Just blocked. Genuinely cried that day. Which is still embarrassing to admit because if someone told me they cried because their crush ignored them, I'd probably laugh. 😭
Then again, I also laughed at my own suffering a few times, so maybe I'm not qualified enough to judge. The weird thing is that I don't think blocking itself messed me up as much as uncertainty. Because now my brain GENUINELY treats uncertainty like a natural disaster. Someone acts slightly different? I freak out. Someone smiles at me? Congratulations, you just made me overthink harder. 😭

I hate uncertainty.

And somehow my crush became the final boss of uncertainty. The funny part is tht I'm REALLY calm person. I've literally talked to investors, developers, I can discuss projects, I can explain how AI works to people, But when my crush smiles at me, my confidence collapses HARD.

I cannot deal with this crush shit anymore, okay? I saw picture of her on Instagram the other day, and I thought I was having an allergic reaction. I cannot focus, like my brain goes dumb dumb caveman mode when I'm talking to her (not rlly talking anymore nowadays). I am a man of superior intellect and yet, I am stunned by this phenomenon. BUT that's part of human experience, but idk bro will she like me back? 😭

The other day she smiled at me in coaching.

I immediately looked at the ceiling.

Why?

No idea.

There was absolutely nothing up there.

Ceiling wasn't smiling at me.

Ceiling wasn't my crush

I also have this amazing ability where I can act confident for approximately six seconds. After that, my confidence experiences the 1929 Wall Street Crash. And despite all of this, despite the overthinking, despite the confusion, despite the fact ion know why I got blocked...

I'm still waiting for an answer...
Not because I think everything is magically gonna make sense one day, I just hate unfinished stuff. And unfortunately for me, this one is still unfinished. To this day, I still don't know what went wrong.

Maybe it was my fault.
Maybe it wasn't.
Maybe she lost interest.
Maybe someone said false stuff to her about me which isn't true.
Maybe she's just trying to get rid of me.
Maybe there's a HUGE misunderstanding between us.
Maybe she saw me differently than I saw myself because I'm an introvert and I'm really bad at expressing my feelings.

I genuinely don't know. I just wish I had an answer. And tbh, I still hope she'll come back one day.
